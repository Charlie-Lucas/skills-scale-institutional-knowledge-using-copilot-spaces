# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management knowledge base. This documentation provides a comprehensive guide to how OctoAcme runs cross-functional projects that deliver product features, services, and integrations. These docs are designed to help new team members quickly understand our approach, establish clear roles and responsibilities, and ensure consistent delivery practices across all projects.

OctoAcme follows a clear, iterative lifecycle for project delivery that emphasizes customer value, psychological safety, and data-informed decisions. Our process spans five key phases: **Initiation** establishes the business need and stakeholder alignment; **Planning** breaks work into actionable backlogs with clear acceptance criteria; **Execution & Tracking** manages day-to-day progress through standups, PRs, and quality gates; **Release & Deployment** ensures controlled, observable rollouts with rollback plans; and **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement** captures learnings and converts them into actionable improvements. Each phase has defined deliverables, decision gates, and quality standards to maintain transparency and minimize risk.

Core roles drive accountability and focus: **Project Managers** coordinate delivery, schedules, risks, and communications; **Product Managers** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure success; **Developers** implement features while maintaining quality and testability; and **QA/Testing** validates acceptance criteria. Communication is structured and regular—daily standups keep the team aligned on progress and blockers, weekly syncs between PM and Product Lead address risks and decisions, and monthly stakeholder updates maintain transparency. All projects maintain key artifacts including a Project Charter, Risk Register, backlog with Definition of Done, and retrospective action items.

Quality assurance is integrated throughout the workflow. Pull requests require automated tests, linting, and security scans before review. Teams follow a clear project board workflow (Backlog → Ready → In Progress → In Review → QA → Done) with small, testable increments. Risk management is proactive—risks are identified during planning, assessed for impact and likelihood, mitigated through action plans, and monitored in weekly syncs. The emphasis on iterative delivery, clear ownership, and continuous improvement ensures that projects deliver reliable customer value while building institutional knowledge that scales across the organization.
